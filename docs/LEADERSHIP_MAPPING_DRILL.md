# Leadership Round — 20-Minute Mapping Drill (No Answers)

> Pure recognition training. The flow for every question is:
> 1. **INTERVIEWER** (Daniel) fires a behavioral question — order shuffled across all stories.
> 2. **COACH** (Karen) names the signal word and the story bucket.
> 3. **Long silence** — you answer out loud yourself. No model answer is played.
>
> The buckets (reused from Execution):
> 1 Capital One · 2 Siri HomePod · 3 Scope change · 4 Cyber Guardrail · 5 Feature-vs-Debt · 6 Cross-tenant incident · 7 Burnout.
> Supporting stories: Apple observability · Apple KG · Broadcom kill · Fiserv feature store.

---

## INTRO

**COACH:** This is the Leadership mapping drill. Same game as Execution — I ask, I name the signal and the story, then I go quiet and you answer out loud. The whole point is to hear a behavioral question and instantly know which of your stories it belongs to. Leadership just asks the same seven stories from a different angle — influence, judgment, talent, and business value. Ready? Here we go.
