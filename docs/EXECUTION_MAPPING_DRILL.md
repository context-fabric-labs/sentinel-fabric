# Execution Round — 15-Minute Mapping Drill (No Answers)

> Pure recognition training. The flow for every question is:
> 1. **INTERVIEWER** (Daniel) fires a question — order shuffled across all 7 stories.
> 2. **COACH** (Karen) names the signal word and the story bucket.
> 3. **Long silence** — you answer out loud yourself. No model answer is played.
>
> The 7 buckets: 1 Cross-org · 2 Ambiguity · 3 Scope · 4 Conflict · 5 Debt · 6 Incident · 7 Burnout.

---

## INTRO

**COACH:** This is a mapping drill. I'll ask, then I'll name the signal and the story bucket — and then I go quiet. You answer out loud. The whole game is hearing the question and knowing instantly which of your seven stories it belongs to. Ready? Here we go.

---

**INTERVIEWER:** Build an AI platform for the org. There are no real requirements yet — just make it good. Where do you start?
**COACH:** Signal: "no requirements." That's Ambiguity — Story 2, Siri HomePod. Your turn.

**INTERVIEWER:** A P1 security vulnerability just landed in your platform. Walk me through it.
**COACH:** Signal: "P1 security." That's an Incident — Story 6, the cross-tenant leak. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** Your team keeps getting pulled between platform work and product features. How do you balance it?
**COACH:** Signal: "platform versus features." That's Debt — Story 5. Remember: same currency. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** A cross-functional dependency is blocking you and the other team doesn't care about your deadline.
**COACH:** Signal: "dependency, other team doesn't care." That's Cross-org — Story 1. Shrink the ask. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** A senior leader has a pet project you think is the wrong call. How do you say no?
**COACH:** Signal: "say no to a senior leader." That's Conflict — Story 4. Never a flat no. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** How do you protect your team's wellbeing during a high-pressure crunch?
**COACH:** Signal: "wellbeing, high pressure." That's Burnout — Story 7. Structural, not just empathy. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** Your roadmap says twelve months. A VP wants it in six.
**COACH:** Signal: "twelve months into six." Timeline cut — that's Scope — Story 3. Options with tradeoffs. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** You've got work across five teams with real dependencies. How do you sequence it?
**COACH:** Signal: "five teams, dependencies, sequence." Cross-org — Story 1. The DAG and interface contracts. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** You're asked to do something technically you believe is the wrong approach.
**COACH:** Signal: "wrong approach." Conflict — Story 4. Resolve it empirically with a bake-off. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** No active incident, but you want to invest in reliability. Justify it.
**COACH:** Signal: "no active incident, reliability." Still Incident-family — Story 6. Make the latent risk visible. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** Classic scope creep — the asks keep growing.
**COACH:** Signal: "scope creep." Scope — Story 3. Yes, and here's the cost. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** A technology choice where the right answer isn't obvious.
**COACH:** Signal: "tech choice, not obvious." Ambiguity in disguise — Story 2. Weighted criteria, prototype the top two. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** You inherit a team burned by a past migration and they don't trust you.
**COACH:** Signal: "burned team, no trust." Conflict with a Care flavor — Story 4. Start small, give them control. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** You're the bottleneck across multiple teams — everything routes through you.
**COACH:** Signal: "you're the bottleneck." Cross-org — Story 1. Turn yourself into a documented contract. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** Estimate a project you've never done before.
**COACH:** Signal: "estimate the unknown." Ambiguity — Story 2. Decompose by uncertainty, give a range, SPIKE the biggest unknown. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** When do you stop investing in a failing project?
**COACH:** Signal: "stop a failing project." Incident-family — Story 6. Kill criteria defined before you deploy. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** Your manager disagrees with your prioritization.
**COACH:** Signal: "manager disagrees." Conflict — Story 4. Find the root disagreement, then disagree and commit. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** Requirements changed when you were sixty percent done.
**COACH:** Signal: "requirements changed mid-flight." Scope — Story 3. Does the work done still serve the new goal? Rebaseline. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** You have a big vision but only two engineers.
**COACH:** Signal: "big vision, scarce resources." Constraint — Story 6. Cut scope to prove feasibility, earn the headcount. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** Thirty requests from different teams. How do you prioritize?
**COACH:** Signal: "thirty competing requests." Debt and prioritization — Story 5. Public force-rank with a rubric. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** Everyone says their request is "urgent."
**COACH:** Signal: "everything's urgent." Debt — Story 5. Define urgent publicly: revenue per hour or SLA breach. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** How do you report progress on a multi-quarter project to executives?
**COACH:** Signal: "report to executives." Cross-org — Story 1. One-page readout: milestones, dollars, decisions needed. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** A stakeholder keeps changing priorities on you.
**COACH:** Signal: "stakeholder changes priorities." Conflict — Story 4. Make the cost of churn visible, batch the reviews. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** You're going to miss a deadline. What do you do?
**COACH:** Signal: "going to miss a deadline." Conflict and trust — Story 4. Communicate early with diagnosis plus options. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** How do you maintain velocity through a leadership transition?
**COACH:** Signal: "leadership transition." Conflict-family — Story 4. Keep executing, protect the team, brief the new leader fast. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** Two equally important stakeholders want different things from you.
**COACH:** Signal: "competing equal requests." Conflict — Story 4. Find the synergy, sequence foundation first. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** A migration that has to happen with zero downtime.
**COACH:** Signal: "zero-downtime migration." Incident-family — Story 6. Dual-read behind a flag, automated rollback. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** How do you onboard a new team onto your platform?
**COACH:** Signal: "onboard a new team." Conflict and adoption — Story 4. Treat consuming teams as customers — golden path, tiered support. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** A new VP wants to modernize everything at once.
**COACH:** Signal: "new VP, modernize everything." Ambiguity — Story 2. Start with their target outcome, propose a small first step. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** A project three engineers already failed to complete.
**COACH:** Signal: "failed three times." Ambiguity and ownership — Story 2. Diagnose whether it's technical or organizational; change the approach. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** How do you know when you have enough information to decide?
**COACH:** Signal: "enough info to decide." Ambiguity — Story 2. Reversible decide fast, irreversible run a POC. Go.

**INTERVIEWER:** How do you build a roadmap for a platform serving twenty-plus teams?
**COACH:** Signal: "roadmap for twenty-plus teams." Cross-org — Story 1. Each milestone must be independently valuable. Go.

---

## OUTRO

**COACH:** That's the full set. If you named the bucket before I did most of the time, you're ready — because once you know the story, the answer's already in your head. Run it again and beat me to it every time.
